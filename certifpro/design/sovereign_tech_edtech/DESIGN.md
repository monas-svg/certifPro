## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a rigorous, high-trust digital ecosystem tailored for Francophone Africa’s burgeoning tech tier (Cameroon, CEMAC, and broader West/Central Africa). Rejecting fleeting design trends such as nebulous purple neon gradients or illegible floating glass cards, the visual language draws heavily from high-reliability fintech architectures (such as Stripe and Wave) fused with premier technical academies (Coursera, Reforge).

The personality is authoritative, institutional, and relentlessly pragmatic. It communicates immediate credibility, upward socioeconomic mobility, and verified competence. Because mobile devices over 3G/variable networks represent the predominant point of access, the experience focuses on rapid parsing, tactile clarity, low mental overhead, and explicit transactional reassurance.

Key visual attributes include:
- **Architectural Rigor & Density:** Crisp geometric structure, clear visual grouping, and deliberate contrast hierarchies that display flawlessly under glaring equatorial sunlight.
- **Transactional Groundedness:** Native integration of local Mobile Money visual cues (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Wave), visible net XAF (FCFA) pricing without hidden markups, and explicit step-by-step confirmation loops.
- **Tangible Authority:** Deep institutional navy substrates paired with electric cyan operational signals, crisp credential emblems, and high-readability typographic tracking.

## Colors

The palette directly anchors the platform in professional governance while driving conversion through decisive, high-contrast action triggers:

- **Primary (`#0A192F` / Deep Navy):** Represents institutional permanence, enterprise reliability, and foundational authority. Used for prominent headings, hero banners, primary structural cards, and high-trust framing elements.
- **Secondary (`#00A3FF` / Electric Cyan):** The primary kinetic signal. Reserved strictly for forward-action calls-to-action (CTAs), progressive indicators, active tabs, and certified tech accents. It retains optimal contrast against dark navy and off-white backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` / Tech Emerald):** Serves as validation confirmation, delivery success badges, and authentic credential endorsements. Paired with `#ECFDF5` for status badge backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` / Slate):** Governs structural body typography, secondary metadata, borders, and muted supporting elements, avoiding the harshness of pure black while ensuring strong contrast ratios.
- **Named Utility Colors:**
  - `solar-amber` (`#F59E0B`): Applied exclusively to high-urgency notifications, career market-value metrics, and limited-seat catalog badges.
  - `momo-mtn` (`#FFCC00`), `om-orange` (`#FF7900`), `wave-blue` (`#1DC3F4`): Dedicated operator signature accents for MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, and Wave checkout modules.
  - `surface-slate` (`#F8FAFC`): The primary default canvas tone to soften eye strain on mobile devices.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** as a unified typographic engine across all display scales, functional copy, and interactive UI nodes. Its contemporary geometric curves maintain clarity on lower-resolution mobile screens and mobile web browsers without rasterization degradation.

Key execution rules:
- **Heading Architecture:** Bold (700) and ExtraBold (800) headline scales provide unmistakable typographic weight, instantly distinguishing career tracks and pack modules at a glance.
- **Readability on Mobile:** `body-md` is standardized at `15px` with a generous `24px` line-height to reduce visual crowding across narrow viewports.
- **Numbers and Currency:** Prices rendered in XAF (FCFA) leverage semi-bold to bold weight classes with tight tabular numerals, preventing horizontal jitter during real-time fee calculations.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Specialized badges (such as certification provider badges or status pills) use `label-sm` with tight kerning (`letter-spacing: +0.04em`) to establish crisp editorial hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured responsive framework optimized for standard vertical smartphone viewport constraints while comfortably expanding into multi-column desktop grids.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout. Margin set to `1rem`, gutter set to `1rem`. Touch targets expand to full width where appropriate to streamline thumb-driven progression.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 2-column catalog grids, 2-column split checkout structures. Margin set to `2rem`.
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 3-column career catalogs, sticky right-hand summary checkout columns, max canvas container fixed at `1200px` for optimal reading scan lines.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 4px base increments. Forms and checkout steps maintain a tight `space-md` (`1rem`) gap between consecutive input tiers to retain immediate context without pushing primary CTA triggers beneath the mobile keyboard fold.

## Elevation & Depth

To prioritize rapid performance across mobile network bandwidths and preserve architectural sharpness, depth is articulated through **tonal surface stacking** and **crisp low-contrast outlines** rather than resource-heavy multi-stop drop shadows.

- **Surface Levels:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (subtle slate ground that softens glare).
  - **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a clean 1px border of `#E2E8F0`.
  - **Layer 2 (Elevated & Active Selection):** Pure White with a 1.5px border of `#00A3FF` and a soft ambient glow (`0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 163, 255, 0.12)`).
  - **Institutional Inversion (Hero / Value Blocks):** Deep Navy (`#0A192F`) background paired with inset boundary borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Shadow Philosophy:** Minimal and crisp. Standard components use an ambient elevation shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances modern product design with corporate stability:

- **Level 2 Roundedness (Base: 8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied uniformly to inputs, action buttons, filter tags, and operational modal dialogs.
- **Cards and Containers (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Applied to career cards, payment selection panels, and certification breakdown matrices.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Reserved strictly for compact metadata items: provider tags (e.g., Coursera, Microsoft), live transaction statuses, and Mobile Money operator identifier badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Background `#00A3FF`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 700. Min-height `52px` on mobile for accessible tap areas. Active states utilize `#0088E8`.
- **Secondary / Institutional:** Background `#0A192F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Used for critical institutional authorizations and manual download resend triggers.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** White background with `#E2E8F0` border and `#0A192F` text; shifts to `#00A3FF` border on hover/focus.

### Mobile Money Provider Radio Cards
- Structured interactive tiles for selecting operators (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Wave).
- Contains operator brand insignia, carrier title, and a explicit radio circle indicator.
- Selected state activates a `2px` solid border in the operator's brand tone or `#00A3FF`, accompanied by a subtle tinted background wash.

### Form Inputs
- Single-column layout on mobile devices.
- Minimum field height of `50px`. 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, transitioning to 2px solid `#00A3FF` on focus with no blurred offsets.
- Clear persistent floating labels and explicit field hints (e.g., *"Vos liens de formation seront envoyés à cet e-mail"*).

### Course & Career Pack Cards
- Structured overview of career track title, total included certifications, market role viability, and upfront XAF pricing.
- Hidden URL indicators: clear visual locks indicating training URLs are securely dispatched post-payment confirmation.
- Direct CTA trigger: *"Obtenir mon pack"* in primary Cyan.

### Badges & Verification Chips
- **Certification Badges:** Deep Navy outline or subtle emerald background (`#ECFDF5`) with `#059669` text, accompanied by a checkmark emblem.
- **Urgency / High Value Badges:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, displaying metrics like *"Forte demande CEMAC"*.
